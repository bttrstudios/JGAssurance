SMS Terms & Conditions

Last Updated: June 19, 2026

Welcome to JG Quality Health Assurance LLC. By accessing or using our services, including receiving SMS communications, you agree to comply with and be bound by these Terms and Conditions. If you do not agree with these terms, please do not engage with our SMS services.

1. SMS Consent Communication

The information (including phone numbers) obtained as part of the SMS consent process will not be shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

By providing your consent to receive SMS communications, you acknowledge and agree to receive text messages from JG Quality Health Assurance LLC at the phone number you provide. Information obtained as part of the SMS consent process will not be shared with third parties.

2. Types of SMS Communications

If you have consented to receive text messages, you may receive SMS communications related to the following:

  • Appointment reminders and scheduling confirmations
  • Follow-up messages regarding your insurance inquiry or coverage options
  • Medicare, ACA, or Social Security planning updates relevant to your situation
  • Enrollment period reminders and important deadline notifications
  • General communications related to your insurance services with our office

3. Message Frequency

Message frequency may vary depending on the type of communication. You may receive up to 4 SMS messages per month regarding your appointments, coverage options, or account status. Frequency may increase during open enrollment periods or when time-sensitive information requires prompt communication.

4. Potential Fees for SMS Messaging

Please note that standard message and data rates may apply, depending on your carrier's pricing plan. These fees may vary if the message is sent domestically or internationally. JG Quality Health Assurance LLC is not responsible for any charges incurred by your mobile carrier.

5. Opt-In Method

You may opt in to receive SMS messages from JG Quality Health Assurance LLC in the following ways:

  • Verbally, during a consultation or phone conversation with Jesse
  • By submitting the online contact form on our website and checking the SMS consent box
  • By filling out a paper consent form at our office

6. Opt-Out Method

You can opt out of receiving SMS messages at any time. To do so, simply reply "STOP" to any SMS message you receive from us. Alternatively, you can contact us directly to request removal from our messaging list. After opting out, you will receive one final confirmation message and no further SMS communications will be sent.
